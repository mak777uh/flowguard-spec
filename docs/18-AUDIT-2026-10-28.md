## What is healthy

**Every metric row carries a command.** 38 of 38. This is the thing M2 exists for, and it holds.

**Card-to-log links are now complete except three.** Before this audit, 21 of 22 cards named a command but not the
log the run produced — the hash lived only in `METRICS.md`, so a reader could not go from a card to its evidence. 18
were linked in this pass; `E-M19`, `E-M20`, `E-M21` remain because their runs were executed as ad-hoc command
sequences that were never captured to a file. That is a real gap: those three cards assert a result whose output
exists only in the transcript.

**The method catches its own errors.** 25 recorded mentions across the tree: 14 "false positive", 7 "found by E-M…",
3 "caught by". Eight of those were the repository catching *itself* — a checker counting prose, a readiness score
crediting a template, a denominator kind with no source, a guard that scanned the wrong tree. That mutation rate is
the most encouraging number in the audit: the apparatus is hostile to its own claims.

## What is wrong, ranked

### 1. Fifteen claims cite artifacts that do not exist (high, known)

`docs/CLAIMS.md` lists 18 claims; 15 name an artifact that was never built. The linter reports them as warnings and
`STATUS-v0.md` publishes the count, so it is disclosed — but disclosed rot is still rot. Either the artifact gets
built, or the claim moves to `withdrawn`. Leaving it in the middle is the pattern this repository criticises in others.

### 2. Three cards assert results with no captured log (high, fixable)

`E-M19`, `E-M20`, `E-M21` ran as command sequences without a log file. Their numbers are reproducible in principle —
the commands are in the card — but "in principle" is exactly what a reader cannot check. Fix: re-run each with output
captured, then link.

### 3. Nine superseded documents are still linked as current material (medium)

`docs/90-DEV-AND-NEXT-ROUND.md`, `docs/97-HACKATHON-PLAN.md`, `PACKET-ROUND6.md`, the v0.2 legacy spec and others are marked superseded in their own headers, and the
README links some of them without that label. A reader follows a link and lands in a document describing a product
that no longer exists. Fix: label at the link, or move to `docs/archive/`.

### 4. Two large screenshots (640KB of 1.78MB) (low)

`login_window.png` and `app_editor.png` are from an abandoned browser-verifier line. They are 36% of the repository
size and describe a feature that was cut. Move to `docs/archive/` or drop.

### 5. The method has no audit cadence (medium)

This is the first whole-repository audit, after 67 commits and 22 experiments. The instruments check *inside* their
own lane; nothing was checking whether the lanes add up. The audit is now a tool — it should run every ten rounds and
its findings should become cards.

## The method audit, in its own terms

The method is: pre-register a threshold, run, publish the result including failures, write a card, record a metric,
keep the guards green, and let an outside party attack the claims.

Measured against its own rules:

| rule | holds? | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| every experiment has a threshold fixed before the run | **yes** | 22 of 22 cards carry `threshold:` |
| failures are published, not rewritten | **yes** | `E-M9` stands at 3/6 with the fix in a separate card |
| every metric has a command | **yes** | 38 of 38 |
| every card links its evidence | **no, 3 gaps** | this audit |
| every claim has an artifact | **no, 15 gaps** | disclosed, not fixed |
| the guards catch the method's own mistakes | **yes, 8 times** | recorded catches |
| an outside party has attacked the claims | **yes** | three independent reviews, one exchange with VecDHT |
| a stranger has reproduced the artifact | **no** | M7, still `absent` |

Six of eight hold. The two that do not are the same shape: **an assertion without its artifact** — a claim pointing
at an unbuilt file, a card pointing at an uncaptured run. The repository's own thesis is that this is the failure mode
that matters, and it is present twice inside the repository.
