SCORING RUBRIC
What judges look for
01
Deep Technical Understanding

Show you know the technology, not just the hype. Judges can smell surface-level takes.

02
Novel Insights

Bring fresh perspectives that challenge conventional thinking. Don't rehash what everyone already knows.

03
Real-World Impact

Address actual problems with actionable solutions. Theory is great; impact is better.

04
Research & Evidence

Back up claims with data, papers, and case studies. Specifics beat generalities every time.

05
Clear Communication

Make complex ideas accessible and engaging. If experts struggle to read it, it won't win.

PRO TIP:
Judges favor posts that cite specific projects, papers, and people. Vague generalizations won't cut it.
